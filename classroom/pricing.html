Billing

Money questions, answered plainly

💳 When am I charged?

Never during the 30-day trial, and never without an email first. The first charge lands the day the trial ends, then monthly or yearly on that date depending on what you picked.

🔄 Can I switch between monthly and yearly?

Any time, in one click. Switching to yearly credits whatever you've already paid for the current month. Switching back takes effect at the end of the year you've paid for.

👥 How do school seats work?

One seat per teacher, minimum five, added or removed whenever staffing changes. Seats added mid-term are pro-rated, so you never pay for a full year of someone who joined in May.

🧾 Do you do refunds?

Yes — if you cancel within 14 days of a charge and haven't used the plan, email us and we'll refund it. If something broke and ruined a lesson, tell us anyway; we'd rather fix it and refund you than argue.

🎓 Is there a discount for small schools?

There is. Schools under 150 students, charities and after-school clubs get School-plan features at Pro pricing. Mention it when you get in touch.
