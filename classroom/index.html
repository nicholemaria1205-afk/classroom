Questions

The things everyone asks

🎒 Do students need an account?

No. They open the join page, type the room code and a display name, and start working. Nothing else is collected about them, and boards are wiped when you close the room unless you choose to save the lesson.

💳 What happens after the free trial?

We email you three days before it ends. If you do nothing, your account drops to the free Starter plan — you keep your saved lessons, you just go back to one room at a time. Nothing is charged without you choosing to continue.

🖥️ Which devices work?

Anything with a modern browser: Chromebooks, iPads, Android tablets, laptops, phones and interactive whiteboards. There is no app to install and nothing for IT to approve.

🏫 Can my school pay by invoice?

Yes. The School plan bills per teacher seat and supports purchase orders, invoices and annual terms. Pick School on the subscribe page and we'll send paperwork rather than a card form.

🚪 How do I cancel?

One button in your dashboard, no phone call, no retention maze. You keep access until the end of the period you've already paid for, and you can export every board first.

🔐 Where does our data live?

Teacher accounts store an email and a password hash. Student boards are tied to a room, not a person, and are deleted with the room. You can export or delete everything from your dashboard at any time.
